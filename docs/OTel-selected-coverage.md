# OTel: Selected Blogs and Media Coverage

Open Telco (OTel) AI is an open ecosystem of telecom models, datasets, benchmarks, and training and inference recipes. OTel’s mission is to give telecom operators the freedom to choose their models, software, hardware, and infrastructure. With contributions from across the industry, OTel has built a shared, open-source AI foundation that enables operators to build, adapt, and deploy AI on their own terms.

OTel AI 1.0 was released on March 2, 2026, at MWC Barcelona 2026, followed by OTel AI 2.0 on July 24, 2026, at AMD Advancing AI 2026. OTel includes more than 35 models, with tens of millions of downloads worldwide.
